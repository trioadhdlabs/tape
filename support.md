# Tape: Trading Journal — Support

## Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: **trioadhdlabs@gmail.com**. Please include your device and iOS or macOS version.

## Common questions

**How do I log a day?**
Open the **Day** tab, tap the day, choose Profit or Loss, enter the amount and, if you like, a note. Tap Save.

**How do I log a swing trade?**
Open the **Swing** tab and tap the day you entered, then the day you exited. (Or tap **+**.) Fill in the ticker, long or short, and the profit or loss. Tap a trade's bar on the calendar to edit or delete it.

**Which month does a swing trade count in?**
The month it closes (its exit date). The same goes for weeks and years in Summary.

**Can I log a trade that's still open?**
Not yet. Tape logs closed trades; add the trade when you exit.

**Why don't I see weekends?**
Weekends are hidden in the Day calendar by default. Turn on **Settings → Show weekends in Day** if you trade crypto or futures.

**How do I change the currency?**
**Settings → Currency.** Amounts are shown in the currency you choose; Tape doesn't convert between currencies.

**Where is my data? Can I move it to a new phone?**
On your device only. It moves with your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) or iPhone-to-iPhone transfer. You can also export everything with **Settings → Export as CSV**.

**I turned on the lock and Face ID isn't working.**
Tape falls back to your device passcode. If you can't unlock, make sure Face ID is set up in the Settings app.
