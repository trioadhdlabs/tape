# Privacy Policy

**Tape: Trading Journal** · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Tape does not collect any data. Not about you, not about your trading, not about how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter — the profit or loss for each day, your swing trades, your notes and your settings — is saved in a file on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no server, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network connections.

Your journal is included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) the same way other app data is. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

## Face ID

If you turn on the lock, Tape asks iOS or macOS to confirm it's you with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded; it never sees your face data or passcode.

## Exporting

If you choose **Export as CSV**, Tape creates a spreadsheet of your entries and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Delete all entries** removes everything from the device. Deleting the app does the same.

## Children

Tape doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
